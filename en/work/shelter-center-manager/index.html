Shelter Center Manager

An Arabic desktop app that replaces dozens of scattered Excel files for a shelter center with 1,300+ people, 300 families and dozens of lists.

Year
2026
My role
Requirements with the center's management, solution design, AI-assisted development, and testing on the real data.
Stack
  • Go
  • JavaScript
  • Excel (OOXML)
  • AES-GCM
  • RTL

Private code — live demo on request

The problem

The center's data lived in dozens of Excel files: a list of people, lists of orphans, widows, children and people with disabilities, and lists that each organization asks for in its own format. Changing one person meant finding them in every file by hand, and mistakes in ID numbers and dates kept repeating.

The solution

One record per person, with an independent membership in each list. The app suggests lists that fit each list's conditions; the user always makes the decision.

  • Imports Excel files as they are, and exports them in their exact original format: columns, widths and formulas.
  • Instant search that handles Arabic spelling differences.
  • Family events: death and martyrdom, making a member head of the family, with a preview of every change before it's applied.
  • A full operations log with undo for any operation, and automatic backups.
  • Staff accounts with different permissions, and phone access.

Privacy

The data is sensitive, so it stays on the center's computer in an AES-GCM encrypted database. Internet access goes through an encrypted tunnel; no data is stored on any outside server.
