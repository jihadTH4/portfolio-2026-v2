The idea

Credit purchases reach me as WhatsApp messages in many shapes, and I used to copy them by hand. The app reads the message and understands its context — merchant, items, quantities and units, prices, total — then fills the form for me to check before saving.

  • General payments, or payments against specific items.
  • A statement per merchant, and Excel export.
  • A trash bin with restore, and an encrypted database on the device.
